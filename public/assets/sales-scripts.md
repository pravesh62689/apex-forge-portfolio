# AGENCY OUTREACH SCRIPTS

## 1. The WhatsApp "Soft Pitch" (High Conversion)
*Context: Send to businesses with a Google Map listing but no website.*

"Hi [Owner Name], this is Pravesh, a local web developer right here in Noida. I was looking for [Category, e.g., Dental Clinics] near [Area, e.g., Sector 62] and found your Google listing. You have great reviews! 

I noticed you don't have a website attached to the profile. Customers usually prefer to check a quick website for services and timings before calling. I created a quick, mobile-friendly demo website tailored for your business. Would you be open to seeing it? No obligations."

## 2. The Cold Call Framework
**Intro:** "Hi, am I speaking with the owner or manager?"
**Hook:** "I'm Pravesh. I help local businesses in [City] get more inquiries from Google. I was actually looking at your Google Maps profile—you guys have a solid [X.X] rating, but I noticed there’s no website link for customers to view your services or book easily."
**Value:** "I build fast, one-page websites specifically for [Industry] that load instantly on mobile and route customers straight to your WhatsApp. It takes me 48 hours to set up."
**Close:** "Can I send a free demo link to your WhatsApp so you can see exactly how it would look for [Business Name]?"

## 3. Objection Handling

**Objection:** "We already have a Google listing, we don't need a website."
**Response:** "Your Google listing is exactly how people find you, which is great. But the website is how they *choose* you over the competitor down the street. A clean website with your pricing, FAQs, and a direct WhatsApp button builds immediate trust. Think of Google as your storefront sign, and the website as your receptionist."

**Objection:** "We don't have the budget right now."
**Response:** "I completely understand. That's actually why I don't charge ₹50,000 like traditional agencies. My starter package is just ₹6,000. It's a one-time setup that pays for itself the moment it brings in just one or two extra [customers/clients/patients]."
